Frequently asked questions

How does matching work?

You'll only see profiles of people who train at the same gym as you and who have opted in to being discovered. If you and another member both express interest, you match, and a chat opens up between you.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the person's profile or your chat with them, then tap the "..." menu and choose Report or Block. Reported accounts are reviewed by our team, and we take action on any account that violates our community guidelines, including permanent removal.

How do I delete my account and my data?

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This removes your profile, photos, matches, and messages from Gym Match. If you're unable to access the app, email us at contact@creativedevelopment.co from your registered email and we will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

Why does Gym Match need my location?

We use location only to verify which gym you attend, so that matches are limited to people from your actual gym. We do not display your precise location to other users. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I'm having trouble logging in or verifying my gym

Make sure you're using the latest version of the app and that location services are enabled for Gym Match in your device Settings. If your gym isn't recognized, email us with your gym's name and address and we'll look into adding it.

The app is crashing or not working correctly

Please try updating to the latest version from the App Store and restarting your device. If the issue continues, email us at contact@creativedevelopment.co with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened — this helps us fix it quickly.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, select Gym Match, and change or cancel your plan there. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple directly.

I have a safety concern about someone I matched with

Your safety comes first. Block and report the user immediately from their profile or your chat, and email us with details so our team can investigate. If you are in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services first.

Safety & community guidelines

Gym Match has zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, impersonation, or any behavior that makes another member feel unsafe. Every profile and conversation includes a one-tap option to block or report, and our team reviews reports promptly and takes action, including permanent bans, where warranted.

For more on how we collect, use, and protect your information, see our Privacy Policy.
