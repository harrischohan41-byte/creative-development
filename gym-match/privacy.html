Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Gym Match collects, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have.

Last updated: July 11, 2026 · Effective for Gym Match, operated by Creative Developments

1. Who we are

Gym Match is developed and operated by Creative Developments ("Gym Match," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy applies to the Gym Match iOS app and any related services. If you have questions, contact us at contact@creativedevelopment.co.

2. Who can use Gym Match

Gym Match is intended for adults only. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we remove accounts and data if we learn a user does not meet this requirement.

3. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use Gym Match:

  • Account information: name, email address, date of birth, gender, and login credentials.
  • Profile content: photos, bio, interests, workout preferences, and whether you're looking to connect for training, dating, or both.
  • Gym & location information: the gym you verify as your home gym, and approximate location used only to confirm gym attendance. We do not display your precise, real-time location to other users.
  • Matches, likes, and messages: the people you view, like, match, or chat with within the app, and the content of those messages.
  • Device & usage information: device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and general usage analytics (e.g., which features you use), collected to keep the app reliable and to improve it.
  • Reports & safety information: any reports, blocks, or support requests you submit or that are submitted about you, used to review and enforce our community guidelines.

4. How we use your information

  • To operate core features: verifying your gym, showing you relevant profiles, and enabling mutual matching and messaging.
  • To keep Gym Match safe: reviewing reports, detecting fake or abusive accounts, and enforcing our community guidelines.
  • To provide support when you contact us.
  • To maintain and improve the app, including fixing bugs and understanding aggregate usage trends.
  • To communicate with you about your account, matches, or important updates to our policies.

We do not use your information to make decisions that are unrelated to providing and improving Gym Match, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. How information is shared

  • With other users: your profile (photos, bio, first name, workout/dating intent) is visible to other members of your gym who use the app. Your exact location and contact details are never shared with other users.
  • With service providers: we share limited data with trusted providers who help us run Gym Match, such as cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support tools, under agreements that restrict their use of your data.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Gym Match, our users, or the public.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties or use it for third-party advertising.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access & correction: you can view and edit most of your profile information directly in the app.
  • Visibility: you can pause your profile at any time to stop appearing to other members.
  • Blocking & reporting: you can block or report any user directly from their profile or a chat.
  • Deletion: you can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time via Settings > Account > Delete Account in the app, or by emailing contact@creativedevelopment.co. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data, such as requesting a copy of your data or restricting certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain limited information for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, records related to a safety report).

8. Data security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to continually improve our safeguards.

9. Safety notice

Gym Match facilitates real-world, in-person meetings. Please use good judgment: meet in public places, tell a friend where you're going, and use the in-app reporting and blocking tools if anything feels wrong. We review all reports and enforce zero tolerance for harassment, abuse, or impersonation.

10. International users

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you live. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at contact@creativedevelopment.co.
