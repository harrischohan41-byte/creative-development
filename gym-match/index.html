Built for real gyms, real people

A few things that make Gym Match different from a generic dating app.

Gym-verified profiles

Members confirm the specific gym they attend, so matches are always people from your actual gym community.

Mutual match only

Messaging only unlocks once both people match. No unsolicited messages, ever.

Workout or date — you choose

Set your intent: training partner, date, or open to either. Matches are shown your preference upfront.

Block & report, built in

Every profile and chat has one-tap reporting and blocking, reviewed by our team.

Trust & Safety

Gym Match is built around real-world, in-person meetings, so we take safety seriously:

  • Every user can block or report another user directly from a profile or chat.
  • We have zero tolerance for harassment, abuse, or impersonation, and act on reports promptly.
  • Location is only ever used to verify your gym — it is never shown to other users precisely.
  • You control your visibility and can pause or delete your profile at any time.

Read more in our Privacy Policy, or contact us via the Support page for any safety concern.
