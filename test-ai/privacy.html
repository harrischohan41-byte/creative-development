Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Test AI collects, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have.

Not medical advice

Test AI provides general, educational estimates about food and testosterone based on published nutrition research. It does not measure your actual hormone levels, and it is not a medical device, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your health.

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Effective for Test AI, operated by Creative Developments

1. Who we are

Test AI is developed and operated by Creative Developments ("Test AI," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy applies to the Test AI iOS app and any related services. If you have questions, contact us at contact@creativedevelopment.co.

2. Who can use Test AI

Test AI is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16, and we remove accounts and data if we learn a user does not meet this requirement.

3. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use Test AI:

  • Account information: name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Scan data: photos of food you scan, barcodes you scan, and the food items identified from them.
  • Score & health-interest data: your Testosterone Score history, logged meals, and any goals or preferences you set in the app. This reflects your food choices, not any medical or biometric measurement.
  • Camera access: used only at the moment you scan a food item or barcode; we do not access your camera in the background.
  • Device & usage information: device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and general usage analytics (e.g., which features you use), collected to keep the app reliable and to improve it.
  • Support requests: any messages, reports, or corrections you submit to us.

4. How we use your information

  • To operate core features: identifying scanned food, calculating your Testosterone Score, and showing your history and trends.
  • To improve the accuracy of food recognition and scoring over time.
  • To provide support when you contact us.
  • To maintain and improve the app, including fixing bugs and understanding aggregate usage trends.
  • To communicate with you about your account or important updates to our policies.

We do not use your information to make decisions unrelated to providing and improving Test AI, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. How information is shared

  • With service providers: we share limited data with trusted providers who help us run Test AI, such as cloud hosting, food/nutrition databases, analytics, and customer support tools, under agreements that restrict their use of your data.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Test AI, our users, or the public.
  • Your scan history and score are never shown to other users — they are visible only to you.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties or use it for third-party advertising.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access & correction: you can view your scan history, score history, and profile information directly in the app.
  • Reporting corrections: you can flag a food or score result you believe is inaccurate directly from the result screen.
  • Deletion: you can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time via Settings > Account > Delete Account in the app, or by emailing contact@creativedevelopment.co. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data, such as requesting a copy of your data or restricting certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain limited information for legal or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Data security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to continually improve our safeguards.

9. Not medical advice

Test AI's Testosterone Score and food insights are general, educational estimates based on published nutrition research. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and Test AI does not measure your actual hormone levels. Always seek the advice of a physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or your hormone health.

10. International users

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you live. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at contact@creativedevelopment.co.
