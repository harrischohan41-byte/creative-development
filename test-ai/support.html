Frequently asked questions

How does Test AI work?

Point your camera at a food item, meal, or barcode and Test AI identifies it, then shows you its estimated impact on testosterone — good, neutral, or bad — along with a breakdown of the nutrients and compounds behind that score.

How is my Testosterone Score calculated?

Your score is calculated from the foods you scan and log over time, weighted by nutrients and compounds commonly associated with testosterone support (like zinc, healthy fats, and protein) versus those associated with disruption (like excess sugar, alcohol, or certain processed ingredients). The score updates as you log more meals, so it reflects your recent eating pattern rather than a single food.

Is Test AI medical advice?

No. Test AI provides general, educational estimates based on published nutrition research — it does not measure your actual hormone levels and is not a medical device, diagnosis, or treatment. Always talk to a doctor before making health decisions, especially around hormones.

A food I scanned shows the wrong result

Food and barcode databases aren't perfect. If a scan looks wrong, use the "Report an issue" button on the result screen, or email us with the food name and a photo of what you scanned — we review corrections regularly.

How do I delete my account and my data?

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This removes your scan history, score history, and profile from Test AI. If you're unable to access the app, email us at contact@creativedevelopment.co from your registered email and we will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

The camera isn't recognizing my food or a barcode

Make sure you're using the latest version of the app, that camera permissions are enabled for Test AI in your device Settings, and that there's good lighting. For packaged foods, scanning the barcode directly is usually more reliable than scanning the food itself.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, select Test AI, and change or cancel your plan there. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple directly.

The app is crashing or not working correctly

Please try updating to the latest version from the App Store and restarting your device. If the issue continues, email us at contact@creativedevelopment.co with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened — this helps us fix it quickly.
